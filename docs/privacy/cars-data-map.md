# Cars data map

Personal data in buses, rides and car rental (ADR-049 to ADR-053): what is collected, why, where
it lives, how it is protected, who can see it, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and
what export and account deletion do with it.

This page summarises; the sources of truth are:

- `apps/api/src/privacy/data-registry.ts`: export section, deletion treatment and retention for
  every table. A new table fails compilation and `data-registry.spec.ts` until it has an entry.
- `docs/security/data-classification.md`: the classification and protection of each kind of
  data.
- `docs/security/access-control.md`: which role or permission reads what.
- `docs/security/external-communications.md`: what each provider receives.
- `apps/api/src/privacy/retention.service.ts`: the retention periods in days, and the sweeps.

Legal bases follow the platform's privacy notice (an open owner question): contract for booking
and fulfilment, legal obligation for financial records, legitimate interest for safety and fraud
records, and consent for anything optional.

## Not collected

- No card data: payments are hosted checkout only (SAQ-A).
- No device location from the app yet (`expo-location` is deferred, `docs/phases/backlog.md`):
  drivers choose a pick-up point, and SOS from the app is sent without a position.
- No contacts, photos library or microphone. The camera is used only on the operator scan
  screen, as a barcode scanner.
- No precise positions in logs or metrics: attributes are outcomes and ids from fixed lists
  (`docs/security/logging-inventory.md`); PINs, share tokens, QR payloads and NINs are in
  `SENSITIVE_LOG_FIELDS`.

## Rights requests

- **Export** (`POST /v1/me/data-export`, after re-authentication): the `driving`, `rides`,
  `rentals`, `emergencyContacts`, `bookings` and `profile` sections above. A driver's own NIN and
  licence number are included in full (it is their own data, behind re-authentication); other people's data, such as a driver's details on a rider's
  export, is limited to what the rider saw.
- **Deletion** (`POST /v1/me/deletion`, after re-authentication): answers 409 with `blockers` while a ride is live
  (`booking_in_progress`) or earnings or a payout are unsettled (`wallet_balance`); otherwise
  applies the treatments above in one transaction and keeps financial and safety records
  without contact data.
- **Correction:** drivers replace documents, add vehicles and change bank details in driver
  mode; anything else goes through support.
