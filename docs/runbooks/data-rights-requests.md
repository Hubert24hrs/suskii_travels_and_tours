# Data rights requests

Use when someone asks to see, correct, delete, move or stop the use of their personal data by
email, phone, post or through support, or when `PrivacyRequestOverdue` fires. Self-service
exports and deletions are answered at once and recorded automatically.

## Record it the day it arrives

1. In the console, **Privacy**, **Requests**, record the request: what they ask (access,
   rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, portability), where they live (the
   jurisdiction sets the due time: Kenya 7 days for access and 14 for rectification and erasure,
   GDPR one month, an internal 30 days elsewhere), how it arrived and when, and the account or
   the email address to answer. The due time is the date it arrived, not the date it was
   recorded.
2. **Check identity** before giving or changing anything: the request must come from the
   account's verified email or phone, or the person signs in. Never send data to an address that
   is not on the account.

## Answer it

| Request       | How                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Access        | Ask them to use Account, Download my data (re-authentication); for someone without an account, export their booking from the console and send it encrypted |
| Rectification | Profile and saved travellers are editable by them; booking passenger details through the booking's support flow                                            |
| Erasure       | Account, Delete account; it refuses while something is unsettled and says what (booking, refund, wallet)                                                   |
| Objection     | Marketing: preferences or the unsubscribe link stop it at once; other processing: ask the privacy officer                                                  |
| Portability   | The data export is JSON, machine-readable                                                                                                                  |

## Close it

Close the request as **completed**, or **refused** with the reason (identity not verified,
manifestly unfounded, a legal obligation to keep the data, no data held). Tell the person the
outcome and how to complain to their regulator. The register keeps the dates and reasons for
three years, never the content of the request.

Obligations and sources: [privacy obligations per country](../compliance/privacy-obligations.md).
