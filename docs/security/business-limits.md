# Business limits and resource-heavy functions

The limits the platform enforces per person and across the application, and how expensive
features are kept available (ASVS V2.1.3, V15.1.3). Values come from code constants or
configuration, and the source is named in each row. Change a limit in its source, then update
this page.

## Request limits

Redis sliding windows (`apps/api/src/rate-limit`). A refused request gets `429` with
`Retry-After` and is logged at `warn`. Limits keyed by email or phone hash the value with HMAC
first. If Redis is unreachable the route limiter fails open and logs a warning, so browsing and
search stay up. Sign-in locks and one-time codes also live in Redis, but they fail closed:
without Redis, credential checks answer with an error instead of skipping the lock.

## Business rules

| Rule                        | Limit                                                                                                                                           | Source                                                   |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Travellers per booking      | 9, with no more infants than adults                                                                                                             | `MAX_TRAVELLERS`, `traveller-rules.ts`                   |
| Flight search               | Up to 5 legs, 361 days ahead, no dates in the past                                                                                              | `search-limits.ts`                                       |
| Hotel search                | Up to 8 rooms, 6 adults and 4 children a room, 30 nights                                                                                        | `search-limits.ts`                                       |
| Saved travellers            | 20 per account                                                                                                                                  | `MAX_SAVED_TRAVELLERS`                                   |
| Price alerts                | 10 active per account; checked every 6 hours; a notice needs a 5% drop                                                                          | `MAX_PRICE_ALERTS`, `PRICE_ALERT_*`                      |
| Sessions                    | 10 per customer and 3 per staff member; the oldest goes first, never the current one                                                            | `MAX_ACTIVE_SESSIONS`, `STAFF_MAX_ACTIVE_SESSIONS`       |
| Session lifetime            | Customers: 90 days absolute, 30 days idle. Staff: 12 hours absolute, 30 minutes idle                                                            | `session.service.ts`, `STAFF_SESSION_*`                  |
| Quotes and search results   | Search results expire after 30 minutes (`410`); prices are re-checked before payment, and a change needs the traveller's consent                | ADR-009, ADR-014                                         |
| Unpaid bookings             | Expire when the payment window or fare deadline passes                                                                                          | `PAYMENT_SESSION_TTL_MINUTES`, worker                    |
| Installments                | Up to 3 payments, 30% deposit, at least 24 hours apart; flights with supplier holds only, no paid extras                                        | `INSTALLMENT_*`                                          |
| Seats on tours and packages | Never oversold: a conditional update in the booking transaction (`409 sold-out`)                                                                | `reserveSeats`                                           |
| Bus seats                   | Sold once per departure (partial unique index); sales close 30 minutes before departure; boarding opens 3 hours before and closes 6 hours after | `claimBusSeats`, `SALES_CLOSE_MINUTES`, `boardingWindow` |
| Refunds                     | Never above the source balance; staff refunds need a second approver above the threshold (default: always)                                      | `RefundsService`, `REFUND_APPROVAL_THRESHOLD_NGN`        |
| Captured payments           | At risk score 60 or above, the booking waits for finance. Signals: 5 payments a day, 10 attempts per IP, 3 cards                                | `PAYMENT_RISK_*` (ADR-040)                               |
| Ticketing                   | 6 automatic attempts with backoff, then a refund review                                                                                         | `TICKETING_MAX_ATTEMPTS`                                 |
| Referral rewards            | Zero until the owner sets amounts; 10 qualifying referrals a month per referrer                                                                 | `REFERRAL_*`                                             |
| Prime pricing               | Member benefits never price below supplier cost                                                                                                 | `priceOffer()`                                           |
| Idempotent writes           | One outcome per `Idempotency-Key` for 24 hours                                                                                                  | `idempotency.interceptor.ts`                             |
| Admin reports               | Date ranges up to 366 days                                                                                                                      | `MAX_DASHBOARD_DAYS`                                     |

## Resource-heavy functions

| Function                           | Cost                                                                   | Protection                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Flight and hotel search            | Supplier calls, seconds of latency, supplier quotas                    | Cache-first results in Redis; 12 s budget and 10 s per supplier; circuit breakers; per-IP and daily quotas; partial results instead of failure                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Duffel flight quotes               | A fresh Duffel search per quote (one order per offer request, ADR-062) | 30 quotes a minute per IP (`SEARCH_LIMITS`); runner timeout and circuit breaker; the search result must still be live                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| AI trip search                     | A model call per message (tokens billed by the provider, up to 8 s)    | Off in production unless set; rate limits above; the worst case is reserved against a daily spend cap per user or guest address (`AI_SEARCH_USER_DAILY_MICROUSD`, $0.05) and for everyone (`AI_SEARCH_GLOBAL_DAILY_MICROUSD`, $20) before the call and settled to the actual cost; over a cap it answers 429 and the form still works |
| Booking creation and payment start | Supplier price checks and holds                                        | Per-IP and per-user limits; idempotency keys; holds capped per account; Turnstile or attestation for guests                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Supplier booking (ticketing)       | Up to 45 s per call                                                    | Runs in the worker, not in a request; idempotency key per item; ambiguous timeouts go to review, never a blind retry                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| PDF tickets and vouchers           | CPU for rendering                                                      | Rendered once on confirmation and stored; later downloads serve the stored file                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Visa document upload and scan      | Bandwidth, storage, antivirus CPU                                      | Size limit read after the ownership check; per-application cap; scanning in the worker (30 s timeout, 5 attempts)                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Data export                        | Reads every table for one account                                      | 5 a day per user; re-authentication                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Admin dashboards and reports       | Aggregate queries                                                      | Staff only; date range cap; indexed queries                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Deal and destination refresh       | Supplier searches                                                      | Scheduled in the worker with fixed routes; never triggered by visitors                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Price alert checks                 | Searches per alert                                                     | Batches of 50 every 6 hours, through the search cache                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Retention sweep                    | Deletes across tables                                                  | Daily, in batches of 200                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Ride quotes                        | Routing call per quote (maps provider)                                 | Signed in only; 30 a minute per IP and per user; 5-minute fare; straight-line fallback capped at `RIDE_FALLBACK_MAX_KM`                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Ride matching                      | A proximity query per waiting ride                                     | Worker every 5 s under a Redis lock; GiST index on positions; 200 rides per run; one open offer per ride and per driver                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Driver positions                   | A write per fix, a socket message per live ride                        | One every 5 s per driver (20 a minute with slack); plausibility checks (service area, 50 m/s, five-minute clock skew)                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Ride socket                        | A connection and Redis subscriptions per client                        | 5 connections per user per replica, 20 messages a second, 4 KB messages, 5 s to authenticate, ping every 25 s, closes when the token expires                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| SOS                                | SMS to up to three contacts                                            | 5 a minute per user (never below one a minute, ADR-050); handled in the request, not queued                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Driver applications and documents  | KYC lookups; storage and antivirus                                     | 5 applications an hour per user, one driver per NIN and licence; 20 uploads an hour per user, 40 per driver, size limit after the ownership check                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Rental search and quotes           | An availability query per car; a routing call per delivery             | 60 a minute per IP for search; quotes signed in, 30-day maximum rental, 180-day horizon, delivery within the car's radius                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Rental licence and photo uploads   | Storage and antivirus                                                  | 20 licence uploads an hour per user, size limit after the ownership check; 30 photos per rental, scanned before they count                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Ledger reconciliation              | A provider verification per captured checkout payment                  | Worker hourly; 100 payments, refunds and payouts per run, each checked once; no new provider call after 20 s                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Driver payouts                     | A bank transfer per driver                                             | Worker daily; one provider batch (100) per run; at least `DRIVER_PAYOUT_MIN_MINOR`; one pending payout per driver and currency                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

Capacity is checked by the k6 load test in CI (ADR-045). Locally it handled five times the
spec's peak within the latency thresholds. The `QueueBacklog`, `ApiLatencyHigh` and
`DatabaseConnectionsHigh` alerts watch production (ADR-047).
